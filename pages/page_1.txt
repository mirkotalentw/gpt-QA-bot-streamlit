TW APP Documentation
1 Agenda
The Agenda feature in the Talentwunder app is designed to manage tasks and activities, for 
overview and organization of all candidate-related actions. Users can create tasks or actions, 
which are then categorized and displayed for easy tracking. The Agenda provides three main 
views:
All: A general snapshot of all tasks.
Completed: A list of tasks that have been finished.
Uncompleted: Includes both pending tasks and overdue items for better prioritization.
Users can edit tasks directly and synchronize them with their Google or Outlook calendars to 
stay on top of deadlines and schedules. The feature also includes a detailed view where all task  
information is displayed. This detailed view allows users to sort tasks by due date or Project and  
filter tasks by their type (all tasks, completed, uncompleted), making it easy to focus on what's 
most important. With these capabilities, the Agenda feature simplifies activities overview and 
keeps things organized.
1.1 All Tab
This tab displays all activities and tasks, regardless of their status (completed or uncompleted).
1.2 Completed Tab
This tab shows only completed tasks.
1.3 Uncompleted Tab
Here, only pending and/or overdue tasks are displayed.
1.4 Detail View
The agenda's detail view allows tasks to be sorted by projects or due dates and provides the 
option to filter tasks by status (all, completed, uncompleted).
