Activities:
Tracks tasks, updates, or progress related to the project, displayed chronologically to 
ensure transparency and accountability.
Saved Searches :
Allows users to store search criteria relevant to the project for streamlined sourcing 
efforts in the future.
Notifications:
Provides updates about the project, such as:
oChanges in project status.
oAddition or removal of project members.
oNew comments or updates.
Notifications are timestamped for context.
1.3.2 Job Info Section
The Job Info section focuses on job-specific details and requirements associated with the 
selected project. It includes the following components:
Job Title:
Displays the title of the job role being recruited for (e.g., SAP Consultant).
Location Details :
oCity: Specifies the job’s location at the city level.
oCountry: Indicates the country where the job is based.
oRadius (km): Defines the geographical range around the city for sourcing 
candidates.
Skill Requirements :
oMandatory Skills : Lists essential skills that candidates must possess for the 
role.
oOptional Skills : Includes additional desirable skills that add value but are not 
critical.
Industry:
Specifies the industry or domain relevant to the job role, such as IT or Finance.
Job Description :
A detailed field for describing the job’s responsibilities, expectations, and additional 
context.
Contract Information :
Indicates the type of contract offered, such as permanent, freelance, or fixed-term.
