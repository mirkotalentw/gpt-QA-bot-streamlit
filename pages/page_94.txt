Creating and Managing Job Ads
When you have a vacancy to advertise, you can easily create and manage job ads in the Job 
Ads section. This is done by navigating to the "Job Ads" section, where you'll see the 
"Purchased Job Ads"  table and a "New Job Ad" button.
If no job ads are published, clicking the "Job Ads" sidebar shortcut will automatically direct you 
to the job boards catalog, simplifying the process. If you already have job ads that are active, 
pending or expired you will land on the "Purchased Job Ads"  page.
