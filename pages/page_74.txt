B1.2.3.8 Communication
This filter indicates the availability of contact information in candidate profiles, helping 
recruiters identify immediately reachable candidates.
B1.2.3.8.1 E-Mail
Indicates whether a candidate's profile includes an email address. This filter helps identify:
Directly contactable candidates
Profiles with verified email addresses
Quick-access communication options
B1.2.3.8.2 Phone Number
Shows if a candidate's profile contains a phone number. This filter allows recruiters to:
Find candidates available for immediate phone contact
Identify profiles with complete contact information
Filter for candidates who are more likely to be open to direct approaches
These communication filters are particularly useful when:
Time-sensitive recruitment is required
Direct outreach is preferred
Immediate contact capability is important
Building shortlists of readily contactable candidates
Note: The presence of contact information often indicates a candidate's openness to professional 
opportunities and willingness to be contacted.

This feature assesses the likelihood of candidates being willing to relocate for a position, 
using advanced data analysis and behavioral indicators.
