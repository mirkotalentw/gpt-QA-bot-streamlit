B1.2.1.3.1 IT & Tech All
The "IT & Tech All" option provides a unified search across all technology-focused platforms, 
offering recruiters a comprehensive view of technical talent. This consolidated approach enables:
Evaluation of candidates' technical capabilities across multiple platforms
Assessment of coding practices and programming language expertise
Review of contributions to open-source projects and technical communities
Understanding of candidates' problem-solving approaches and coding style
Discovery of specialists in specific technologies or frameworks
Verification of technical skills through actual project work and peer recognition
This comprehensive search option is particularly valuable when:
Searching for specialists with specific technical expertise
Evaluating candidates' practical coding abilities
Looking for active contributors to technical communities
Seeking evidence of hands-on experience with specific technologies
Needing to assess both technical skills and community involvement
Building specialized technical teams or filling niche technical roles
