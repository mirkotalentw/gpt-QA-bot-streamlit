B1.1.2 Job Title
The Job Title filter is a smart, AI-enhanced search field that goes beyond simple keyword 
matching. Key features include:
Intelligent recognition of job title variations and synonyms
Automatic inclusion of related positions and equivalent roles
Understanding of industry-specific title conventions
Recognition of international job title variations
Suggestion of relevant alternative titles during search
For example, entering "Data Scientist" might also consider:
Related roles like "Data Analyst" or "Machine Learning Engineer"
Industry variations like "AI Researcher" or "Statistical Analyst"
Regional title differences across markets
B1.1.3 City/Radius
The City/Radius filter allows for location-based searching with flexible geographic targeting:
Enter any city name to center your search
Set a custom radius (in kilometers) to define the search area
Includes surrounding communities and suburbs within the specified radius
Considers candidate willingness to relocate within the area
Accounts for commutable distances based on transportation infrastructure
Features:
Autocomplete city name suggestions
Adjustable radius from 0-500 km
Smart recognition of metropolitan areas
Consideration of cross-border regions where applicable
