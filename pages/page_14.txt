Seniority:
Specifies the required experience level for the job, such as junior, mid-level, or senior.
Field of Activity :
Categorizes the job’s functional area or domain, such as logistics, development, or 
operations.
Job Type:
Defines the nature of the job, such as full-time, part-time, or remote.
Sourced Profiles :
Displays all candidates that are in the first step of the recruiting process. Here users can 
see all of the incoming (freshly added) candidates to the project. The ones that made it 
into the longlist and need more attention.  Each entry includes:
oCandidate’s name.
oCurrent professional role.
oSource or platform from which the profile was retrieved.
Comments:
A collaborative space where team members can leave notes or discuss aspects related to 
the job’s requirements and progress.
Profile Recommendations :
Provides a curated list of recommended profiles aligned with the job’s criteria. Each 
recommendation includes:
oCandidate’s name and role.
oLocation and employer.
oSource platform of the candidate profile.
Saved Searches :
Enables users to save specific search criteria for future use, making sourcing more 
efficient.
Notifications:
Displays real-time updates and changes related to the job, such as:
oStatus updates.
oChanges to job details.
oAddition or removal of project members.
1.3.3 Edit Field (Blue Pencil icon)
Click the blue pencil to edit project or job information. Only Project owner can edit the project 
details.
