Matching Score:
Talentwunder uses a Matching Score to help you find the best job boards for your project. This 
score is a star rating based on how well the job board matches your job title and location. The 
score is calculated based on:
Candidate interactions : The engagement of candidates on that job board for similar job  
titles and locations.
Relevance: How relevant the job board is for the specific role you are trying to fill.
The Matching Score is displayed as a star rating on each job board card. A higher score 
means the job board is more likely to attract suitable candidates for your position.
Tabs for Products and Packages
You will find two tabs:
1.Products: Individual job board products are displayed here.
2.Packages: Bundles of job board products with discounted prices are available here.
For convenience, both tabs offer Card View and List View. Products from the same supplier 
are grouped together in both views.
Each product card includes:
Supplier Logo.
Original Price (shown with a strikethrough, if available).
Discounted Price (the lowest available price from the supplier’s group of products).
Matching Score: A star rating based on the Job Title and Location. This score 
represents product popularity for the selected job title, based on candidate interactions 
and engagement on the job board. If a product does not have a matching score, it will be  
omitted from the display.
Modal Window:
Clicking on a product card will open a modal showing all products from that supplier. Here, you’ll  
find details such as:
Ad Type.
Exact Price.
Ad Duration.
Matching Score.
Country
