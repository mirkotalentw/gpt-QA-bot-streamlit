Utility:
The Filter function provides recruiters with a granular approach to finding the right 
candidates for specific roles, helping them focus on the most relevant profiles.
Export Feature
The Export functionality allows users to download candidate data from the Candidate 
Board in a structured format (CSV). This tool is located next to the Filter icon and 
provides a seamless way to extract data for offline analysis or sharing.
