E1.2 Cost of Living Metrics
The Cost of Living Metrics represent a multi-faceted approach to understanding and comparing 
living expenses between locations. This system incorporates various economic indicators, local 
market conditions, and lifestyle factors to provide a holistic view of living costs.
E1.2.1 General
E1.2.1.1 Local Purchasing Power (LPP)  The Local Purchasing Power index is a 
fundamental metric that quantifies the relative buying capacity of a typical salary in a given 
location. This sophisticated measure takes into account average salaries and living costs to 
determine how much people can actually afford in different cities. It serves as a crucial indicator 
for understanding real-world living standards and economic conditions across different locations,  
going beyond simple currency conversions to reflect actual lifestyle possibilities.
E1.2.1.2 Income Comparison  The Income Comparison feature provides a nuanced 
analysis of salary equivalence between locations, accounting for differences in living costs, 
taxation, and economic conditions. This tool helps users understand what salary level would be 
needed in a different location to maintain their current standard of living, making it invaluable 
for career decisions and salary negotiations.
E1.2.1.2.1 Select Currency  The currency selection functionality enables users to view and 
compare financial data in their preferred currency. This feature ensures that users can understand  
costs in familiar terms while maintaining accurate relative values across different economic 
zones.
E1.2.2 Combined Metrics  The Combined Metrics section presents a comprehensive 
overview of key cost indicators, integrating various aspects of daily living expenses. This 
includes consumer prices with and without rent, grocery costs, and dining expenses. The system 
analyzes these factors collectively to provide a more complete understanding of cost differences 
between locations.
E1.2.3 Individual Comparison  The Individual Comparison feature offers detailed, 
category-specific cost analysis across different aspects of daily life:
Housing Costs A thorough examination of residential expenses, including rental costs for 
various apartment types, utility expenses, and property purchase prices. The analysis 
distinguishes between city center and peripheral locations, providing a comprehensive view of 
housing options and their associated costs.
