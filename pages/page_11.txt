Clicking on Finish button completes the project creation and on the next window multiple options  
are presented with three key options to proceed further:
1.Book Job Ads: 
a.Promote your project by booking job advertisements directly through 
Talentwunder on various job boards.  
b.This feature allows you to reach a wider audience by publishing job listings on 
relevant platforms.
2.Start Boolean Search : 
a.Use advanced Boolean search  techniques to find candidates. This option will 
automatically create the boolean search query for user, taking the data specified 
within the project.
b.This option lets you create complex and precise search queries based on the data 
from the project to target the best-matched candidates.
3.Start Smart Search : 
a.Leverage Smart Search to simplify the candidate search process.  
b.This AI-driven feature uses the project data to recommend suitable candidates 
quickly and efficiently.
These options ensure you can easily move forward with your recruitment process, whether by 
promoting the role, searching with precision, or relying on AI to recommend the best candidates.
