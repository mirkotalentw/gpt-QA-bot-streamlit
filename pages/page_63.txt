B1.2.6 Others
The Others category encompasses specialized job platforms, regional employment services, and 
niche professional networks that don't fit into the traditional networking categories. These 
platforms include government employment agencies, regional job markets, and freelance 
communities. Unlike mainstream professional networks, these platforms often provide access to 
local talent pools, specialized workforce segments, and candidates who might not maintain a 
strong presence on major social networks. They are particularly valuable for recruiters seeking 
candidates in specific regions, government-registered job seekers, or specialized freelance 
professionals. These platforms often offer unique insights into candidates' official qualifications, 
regional availability, and specific work arrangements.
B1.2.6.1 Others All
The "Others All" option enables a comprehensive search across these diverse platforms, 
providing recruiters with access to alternative talent pools and specialized candidate databases. 
This integrated approach allows:
Access to government-registered job seekers and officially verified profiles
Discovery of regional talent through local employment agencies
Connection with freelance professionals across various industries
Identification of candidates who prefer traditional job-seeking channels
