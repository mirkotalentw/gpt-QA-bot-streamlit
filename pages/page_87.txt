D1.2.4 Career Score
Evaluates a company's commitment to employee development and career progression through 
six weighted metrics:
 
1. Promotion Loyalty Ratio
- Compares tenure rates between promoted and non-promoted employees
- Indicates fairness in promotion practices
- Shows correlation between loyalty and advancement
- Reflects recognition of long-term employees
 
2. Young Talent Promotion Ratio
- Measures proportion of younger employees receiving promotions
- Shows commitment to developing early-career professionals
- Indicates opportunities for rapid advancement
