3.1.3.2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):  
To set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) in the Talentwunder app, follow these simple steps:
 
1. Download and Install the Authentication App:
   - Download the Google Authenticator app on your mobile device from either the Google Play 
Store (for Android devices) or the Apple App Store (for iOS devices).
2. Scan the QR Code:
   - Open the Google Authenticator app and use the QR code scanning option to scan the QR 
code displayed on the screen. This will link your account with the authenticator app.
3. Verify the Authentication Code:
   - The app will generate a unique 6-digit code that changes every few seconds.
   - Enter this code into the "Authentication Code" field in the app and click Verify Code.
Once verified, your Two-Factor Authentication is successfully enabled. This ensures that in 
addition to your password, you’ll need the unique code from the authenticator app to log in to 
your account. This adds an extra layer of security to protect your data.
The screen in the app ensures that the process is straightforward and intuitive, guiding you step 
by step with clear instructions and providing the QR code directly for easy setup.
