What We Offer (Mandatory)
Tip: Highlight key benefits your company offers, such as financial perks, wellness programs, 
and family support. Tailor these to the priorities of your target candidates. Focus on meaningful 
benefits rather than minor perks.
Contact Information (Mandatory)
Tip: Provide contact information for applicants and clearly state how candidates can reach out 
with any questions they may have.
Job Details Tab
Once you’ve customized the job ad, you’ll proceed to the Job Details tab.
If a project was selected earlier, some fields (like Job Title and Location) will be auto-
populated, and modifications to the chosen project cannot be made at this stage.
Fields required in this step include:
Job Title, City, Country, ZIP Code, Contract Type, Industry, Seniority, Field of 
Activity, Job Type.
Optional fields include:
Company Name, Remote Work options (On-site, Hybrid, Remote), Reference Number  
(Enter a reference number for internal tracking. Some job boards support and display 
this number), Contact Information  (This information will be visible to applicants on 
some job boards. Ensure it is suitable for public display), Salary (If Salary is selected, 
you must provide Minimum and Maximum amounts, Currency, and whether it is monthly 
or yearly) and Keywords.
Validation:
The system ensures all mandatory fields are filled before allowing you to continue. If a field is 
missing, the system will scroll to the missing information and show a validation message.
Country Dropdown :
Select the location where you want the job ad to be published. Accurate selection ensures 
better targeting and visibility on location-focused job boards.
Only locations available in the selected products will appear in the dropdown. If any selected 
product doesn’t support a specific location, a validation pop-up will appear.
