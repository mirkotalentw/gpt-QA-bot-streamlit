B1.2.3.3 Years of Experience
The Years of Experience filter allows recruiters to target candidates based on their 
professional tenure, providing precise control over experience requirements in the search 
process. 
B1.2.3.3.1 From (specify years)
The starting point of the experience range:
Defines the minimum years of relevant experience required
Helps filter out entry-level candidates when seeking more experienced professionals
Ensures candidates have sufficient practical knowledge
Key Features:
Numerical input from 0-50 years
Smart calculation of cumulative experience
Recognition of parallel experience in multiple roles
Consideration of part-time and full-time experience
Understanding of internships and apprenticeships
B1.2.3.3.2 To (specify years)
The upper limit of the experience range:
Sets the maximum years of experience for the search
