 B1.2.5.2.2 Probability of Job Change
Sorts candidates based on their likelihood of being open to new opportunities:
- Higher probability candidates appear first
- Based on behavioral and professional indicators
- Considers current employment duration and activity signals
 B1.2.5.2.3 Probability of Relocation

- Ranks by relocation likelihood
- Considers factors like mobility history
- Takes into account current location versus job location

A powerful feature that allows fine-tuning of AI-generated search expansions after the initial 
search is performed.
 
B1.2.5.2.1 Semantic Expansion for Jobs
The system automatically generates related job titles and relevant skills based on the original job 
title entered. For example, if searching for "Data Scientist," the system might suggest:
- Related titles: Data Analyst, Machine Learning Engineer, AI Specialist
- Associated skills: Python, Machine Learning, Statistical Analysis
