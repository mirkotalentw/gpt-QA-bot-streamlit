D1.2.3 Competitiveness Score  Evaluates a company's ability to attract and retain top 
talent through six key metrics:
1.Boomerang Ratio
Measures proportion of returning employees to total workforce
Indicates strength of company culture and workplace satisfaction
Strong indicator of positive work environment
Shows employee willingness to return after leaving
2.High Potential Employee Ratio
Compares number of high-potential employees to total workforce
Indicates company's ability to attract top talent
Measures quality of workforce
Shows concentration of exceptional performers
3.Retention Growth Index
Numerical indicator tracking employee retention trends over time
Shows improvement or decline in retention rates
Measures effectiveness of retention strategies
Indicates long-term workforce stability
4.Tenured Employee Ratio
Proportion of long-term employees to total workforce
Indicates ability to retain experienced staff
Shows workforce stability
Reflects employee satisfaction and loyalty
5.Ph.D. Ratio
Proportion of employees with doctoral degrees
Indicates academic excellence in workforce
Shows ability to attract highly qualified professionals
Measures advanced expertise level
6.Masters Ratio
Proportion of employees with master's degrees
Shows advanced education level in workforce
Indicates ability to attract qualified professionals
Measures professional expertise level
