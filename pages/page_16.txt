Filter options
The Filter option in the Candidate Board allows users to refine their view of candidates 
by applying various criteria. This tool is essential for narrowing down the list of 
candidates to match specific requirements, enabling more efficient candidate 
management. The filter is accessible via the Filter Icon located at the top of the 
Candidate Board interface.
Key Filter Options:
1.Job Title:
a.Filter candidates based on the job title they are being considered for.
2.Name:
a.Search for specific candidates by their names.
3.Skills:
a.Filter candidates who possess specific skills.
4.Location:
a.Filter candidates based on their geographical location.
5.Industry:
a.Narrow down candidates by their industry of expertise.
6.Years Working:
a.Set a range (e.g., "From X years to Y years") to filter candidates based on 
their work experience.
7.Networks:
a.Choose specific networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Xing) to filter candidates.
8.Language:
a.Add languages to filter candidates who meet specific language 
requirements.
9.Company-Specific Filters :
a.Works At: Filter candidates currently employed at a specific company.
b.Does Not Work At : Exclude candidates employed at certain companies.
c.Previously At: Filter candidates based on their past employment at a 
specific company.
