7.3.9 Job Description
The job description includes the precise job title, main tasks, and required qualifications/profile 
(Must-have and optional skills) a candidate should have. It also includes information on what the  
company offers, employment type, location, and working hours.

7.3.12 Field of Activity

7.3.13 Job Type
Job Type (dropdown) represents a predefined list of job types, allowing users to categorize 
positions based on their employment nature or status in the organization.
