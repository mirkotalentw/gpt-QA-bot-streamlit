When enabled, this feature automatically obfuscates personal identifying information in search 
results and profile pages by:
Hiding candidates' full names
Removing profile pictures
Retaining all professional information such as:
oSkills and qualifications
oWork experience
oEducation
oAchievements
oProfessional certifications
This simple toggle helps maintain candidate privacy while preserving all information relevant to 
the hiring decision. The anonymization applies to both search results and any profile pages 
accessed from that search, ensuring consistent privacy protection throughout the screening 
process.
Note: Personal information remains hidden until the feature is disabled, allowing for objective, 
qualification-based candidate evaluation.
