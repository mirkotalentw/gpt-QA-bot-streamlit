B1.2.3.10 Change Probability
This feature evaluates the likelihood of candidates being open to new opportunities, 
based on various professional and behavioral signals.
B1.2.3.10.1 Slider Function
An intuitive slider control that filters candidates based on their predicted job change probability:
Scale from 0-100%
Lower values: Candidates likely stable in current roles
Higher values: Candidates showing strong indicators of job search activity
Based on factors such as:
oLength of current employment
oRecent profile updates
oProfessional network activity
oIndustry market conditions
oCareer progression timing
Both probability sliders serve as powerful tools for prioritizing candidate outreach and focusing 
recruitment efforts on the most promising prospects. The AI-driven analysis behind these 
features helps recruiters make data-informed decisions about candidate engagement strategies.
