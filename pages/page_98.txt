Header Images:
For the best display, ensure all header images are of the same size. The recommended image 
size for the job ad is 1140 x 456 px (PNG or JPG, up to 2MB).
You can upload up to three header images, either by selecting files or using drag-and-drop.

Job Ad Sections
Introduction
This field is mandatory for content (though the title “Introduction” itself is optional).
Tip: Spark interest by introducing your company, including its size, sector, and mission. Share 
your values and vision to give candidates a sense of your corporate culture. Be authentic and 
avoid buzzwords to build credibility.
Job Title (Mandatory)
Important Tips:
1.Use well-known and industry-standard job titles. Avoid internal company titles that are 
not recognized in the job market.
2.Use gender-neutral titles like “Account Manager (m/f/x)”  to ensure inclusivity.
3.Avoid abbreviations and special characters, as these can negatively impact 
performance.
Your Tasks (Mandatory)
Tip: Clearly outline the main duties and responsibilities in 5-8 bullet points. Start with the most 
important tasks and include details on strategic topics, operational responsibilities, and current 
projects.
Your Profile (Mandatory)
Tip: State the required qualifications, relevant skills, and professional experience. Indicate 
which requirements are essential and which are desirable.
