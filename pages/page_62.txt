B1.2.5 Design
The Design networks category encompasses platforms specifically created for creative 
professionals, visual artists, and design specialists. These networks distinguish themselves by 
their focus on visual portfolios, creative projects, and design achievements. Unlike traditional 
professional networks, Design platforms prioritize visual demonstration of skills through project 
showcases, allowing recruiters to directly assess creative abilities and style. These networks are 
crucial for identifying talent in various design disciplines, from graphic design and UI/UX to 
industrial design and 3D modeling. They provide unique insights into candidates' creative 
processes, technical skills, and artistic evolution through their portfolio of work.
B1.2.5.1 Design All
The "Design All" option provides a comprehensive search across all design-focused platforms, 
offering recruiters a complete overview of creative talent. This unified approach enables:
Review of visual portfolios across multiple creative platforms
Assessment of design skills through actual project work
Understanding of candidates' creative style and artistic direction
Evaluation of technical proficiency in various design tools and methods
Discovery of specialists in specific design disciplines
Analysis of project complexity and creative problem-solving abilities
Insight into collaboration capabilities through team projects
This comprehensive search is particularly valuable when:
Recruiting for creative and design-focused positions
Building multi-disciplinary design teams
Seeking specialists in specific design areas
Looking for evidence of practical design experience
