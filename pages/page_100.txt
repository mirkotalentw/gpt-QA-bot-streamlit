Payment and Order Process
Job Ad Preview
Before finalizing your order, you’ll be shown a preview of your job ad. This is where you can 
review the details and ensure everything is accurate.
Note that each job board may display information differently, and not all details may be shown.
Additional Information for Products
If any product requires more details, these fields will appear next to the job ad preview. If no 
additional information is required, this section remains hidden.
Price Details Overview
The price details section will show:
Items - Supplier logos amd “i” Information about the product
Ad duration.
Price of each product
The total price of all selected products.
Terms and Conditions
Before proceeding with the order, you must accept the Terms and Conditions  of the Job Ad 
Partner.
Link: [https://jobadpartner.de/agb/ ]
Order Now
Click "Order Now" to open a pop-up window where you must enter billing information.
All fields are mandatory except for VAT.
Once confirmed, billing information is sent to the Job Ad Partner, who will then send the  
invoice to your email directly.
Error Handling and FAQs
If any required field is missing or incorrectly filled, the system will automatically scroll to the 
relevant section and display a validation message to help you correct the issue.
