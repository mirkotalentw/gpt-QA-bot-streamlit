For each order, you will find the following information:
Job Title: Taken from the job ad, not from the project it is connected to. This is used as 
the title of the accordion.
Job Board: Displays logos of the job boards where your ad is posted.
More Information: Hover over the “i” icon to see additional details about the purchased 
product.
Status: Indicates if the ad is Active (published) or Closed (expired).
Date Posted: Shows the date when all information was completed and sent to the job 
board. Note that some boards publish instantly, while others may take up to 24 hours to 
go live.
Time Left: Displays the remaining time until the ad expires.
Expiry Date: Shows the exact date the ad will expire. (the accuracy depend on accuracy  
of date posted)
Unpublish: If necessary, you can unpublish your job ad to take it down from the job 
board.
Choosing Job Ads Products and Packages
When you're ready to publish a new job ad, click the "New Job Ad" button to navigate to the 
Job Ads Center. Here, you can select from a range of job boards and packages tailored to your  
needs.
Project Selection:
When selecting a project, the Job Title and Location fields will be automatically populated 
based on the project details. However, you can manually adjust these fields without altering 
your original project selection. These modifications will influence the shop results, ensuring you 
see relevant job boards based on your changes.
Job Boards Search :
A dynamic search bar allows you to find specific job boards quickly, and results adjust as you 
type.
