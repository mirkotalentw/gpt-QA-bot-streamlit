B1.2.1.2 Business
The Business networks category encompasses professional platforms primarily focused on career  
development, business networking, and professional relationships. These networks are essential 
for recruiters as they contain comprehensive professional profiles, including work history, skills, 
and career achievements. Business networks typically attract professionals from all industries, 
making them valuable sources for diverse hiring needs, from entry-level positions to executive 
roles. These platforms often feature built-in communication tools, professional groups, and 
industry-specific communities, enabling effective candidate engagement and relationship 
building.
